The Origin

Born from a theoretical physics concept, Furious Acid was a hypothetical superacid designed to protonate any known element. This concept of dissolving all barriers became the voice of the Purple Rabbit on TikTok in 2020. The project went viral for its "How It’s Made" clarity on chemical procedures—specifically a three-step CBD to THC conversion—which ultimately led to the original channel being banned for being too direct.

The Scientist

Beyond the animation is a Material Science Research Chemist specializing in nanotechnology, nanocrystals, and graphene oxide. With experience spanning hundreds of experiments and government contracts, my work focuses on the intersection of materials and medicinal chemistry. Furious Acid grows through zero paid promotion; it is a 100% organic community of those seeking unfiltered technical truth.
