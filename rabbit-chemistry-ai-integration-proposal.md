# Furious Acid Publishing — Rabbit Chemistry × OpenAI

**Open proposal for scientific software integration**

Website: https://furiousacid.com/rabbitchemistry/

Rabbit Chemistry is a freely available virtual chemistry laboratory developed with assistance from ChatGPT.

Furious Acid Publishing proposes expanding this software through a native ChatGPT connector, integrating scientific simulation, analytical instrumentation, literature research, statistical analysis and scientific report generation.

The proposed expansion includes virtual GC-MS, FTIR, NMR, XRD, microscopy, Monte Carlo modeling and professional scientific visualization.

Rather than replacing existing laboratory software, the integration would connect experimental simulation and AI-assisted research within a unified scientific workflow.

We are interested in technical collaboration, developer support, connector publication and potential inclusion in OpenAI’s scientific and educational ecosystem. We also welcome inquiries from independent developers, researchers and educational technology organizations.

The analytical instrument suite and connector are proposed capabilities, not claims of currently available functionality or an existing partnership.
