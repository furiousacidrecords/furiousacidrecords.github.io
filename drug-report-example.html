[ 01 — THE REPORT BODY ]

What the reader
gets back.

A report is more than pages. It is a way to turn scattered material into a decision, presentation, reference, or next move.

01 / EXECUTIVE READ

What matters first.

A short, direct summary of the question, the finding, and the practical meaning.

02 / METHODS

How it was framed.

Source boundaries, assumptions, analytical categories, and the logic of the investigation.

03 / RESULTS

The evidence.

Tables, figures, observations, comparisons, and visual readouts arranged for clarity.

04 / ANALYSIS

What it means.

Interpretation that connects the evidence back to the original question without losing the reader.

05 / CONCLUSIONS

The clean answer.

A conclusion written so it can stand alone in a meeting, presentation, or private reference file.

06 / APPENDIX

What supports it.

References, supporting notes, and additional context for a reader who wants to go deeper.

[ 02 — HOW IT READS ]

Question.
Evidence.

02Make the evidence legible

03End with a direction
