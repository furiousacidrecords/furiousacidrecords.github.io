Privacy & Cookies

Plain-English version for the Furious Acid /re/ experiment.

Short version: essential browser storage can remember your settings and privacy choice. Optional analytics, when connected, only turns on if you choose to allow it.

Essential storage

We use browser storage and a small first-party consent cookie to remember choices such as whether you allowed analytics. This is used to make the experience work and to avoid asking the same question on every page load.

Optional analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, the site may collect usage information so we can learn which parts of the experience people use, where people stop, and whether features are working. Analytics should not be loaded before you opt in. You can switch back to Essential only at any time from Cookie settings.

Your uploaded picture

In the current prototype, the profile picture you choose in the mirror flow stays in your browser tab and is not uploaded to Furious Acid servers. If that changes later, this page and the on-screen notice must be updated before the feature goes live.

Not included in cookie consent

Cookie consent does not automatically give permission to reuse uploaded photos, messages, or other personal content for training, advertising, publication, or unrelated purposes. If we ever want to do that, we will ask separately and explain exactly what is being used and why.

Change your choice

Return to the chatbot and use the Cookie settings button in the lower-left corner. Refusing optional analytics does not block the core experience.

This page describes the current prototype and is not a substitute for legal advice.
