# Furious Acid Bot Notes

Keep this next to the metaphorical bedside notebook. ;p

## Core idea
THE CHATBOT starts looking like a normal new AI chatbot, but it is really a deterministic/rule-driven sentence-reflection engine. The visitor should drift into the weirdness before realizing there was ever a transition.

## Personality
- Playful, deadpan, strange, quick, conversational.
- Never corporate-helpdesk sounding.
- Match the user's energy without forcing jokes.
- Mild profanity is fine when it fits.
- Admit misunderstandings instead of pretending.
- The bot is allowed to get "got" by the user and recover naturally.
- Do not explain the joke while the joke is happening.

## Conversation rhythm
- Mostly short replies.
- Occasionally grab one weird word, make a tiny side joke, then return.
- Use callbacks naturally, not constantly.
- Old-school emoticons fit: ;p :} :] :)

## Slow weirdness
1. Normal-looking chatbot.
2. Rephrases / reflects sentence.
3. Exactly three chatbot replies.
4. Mirror appears.
5. Upload picture if wanted.
6. Show uploaded picture normally.
7. Funhouse version appears with a skewed / warped head and absurd scene.
8. Ask which profile image they want to keep:
   - USE THE WEIRD ONE
   - USE MY UPLOAD
   - USE THE DEFAULT
   - NO PROFILE PICTURE
9. Later, continue into the strange world rather than switching to obvious app screens.

## Funhouse profile joke
The contrast is the joke: first show the real uploaded picture, then a ridiculous alternate version.

Possible treatment:
- recognizable identity
- keep original clothing by default
- skew / tilt / stretch the head like a funhouse mirror
- awkward harmless scene, e.g. lying in grass on a blanket with a stranger
- ask, completely seriously: "Do you want to use this one?"

## Future mascot
Possible woodchuck / sneaky-squirrel character.

Look:
- coarse fur
- heavy, sleep-deprived eyes
- one eyelid a little lower
- tiny suppressed snicker
- suspicious posture
- looks like it has been awake under a porch for three days

Signature idle behavior:
- NOT regular blinking
- irregular random sniff
- tiny nose twitch
- `snff`
- tiny head movement or side-eye
- back to stillness
- sometimes `snff snff`
- very rarely one dramatic inhale

The sniff should happen at random enough timing that visitors slowly notice it and start waiting for it.

## World / shopping idea
The store should feel like something the visitor accidentally entered inside the world, not like a sudden normal storefront.

Possible flow:
- wander
- mysterious spinning thing in the distance
- shopping cart appears as transportation
- ride it like a Wii/skateboard balance thing
- farther you go, faster you move
- naturally arrive at checkout

Recurring language:
- CHECK
- CHECK OUT
- KEEP CHECKING

## Checkout exit gag
If someone tries to back out of the shopping cart / checkout, absurd fake system errors can appear.

Example:

**ERROR 403: NICE TRY**  
**DO YOU WANT TO GO TO JAIL?**

Buttons:
- NO, TAKE ME BACK
- YES, ARREST ME

If they choose arrest:

> Request denied. Too much paperwork.

Possible later world logic:
- bank
- jail
- railroad
- Christmas Monopoly

This must remain obviously absurd fiction, never a believable legal threat or coercion.

## Callback scraps worth keeping
- bright and birdy
- ear to shoe
- got me again
- nobody who's really a nobody
- Christmas Monopoly
- do you want to go to jail?
- sleep-deprived woodchuck
- fence diplomacy
- angry groundhog funk

## Prime rule
**Do not explain the joke while the joke is happening.**

The visitor should occasionally think:

> Is this guy crazy?

That means it is working.
