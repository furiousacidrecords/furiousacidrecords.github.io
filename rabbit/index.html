Ask the Rabbit anything: why the sky is blue, who discovered penicillin, how fireworks get their colors. It answers in a few lines, and when you're curious to go deeper, it points you to the right Furious Acid book, whether that's eerie art, the story of medicine, or growing plants.

Habla español · Parla italiano. Use the EN / ES / IT buttons in the chat.

The Rabbit is an automated helper, not a person. Short answers to general questions come from Wikipedia's free public API (text by Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA); a question is sent to Wikipedia only to look it up; everything else runs right in your browser. The Rabbit uses no cookies and tracks nothing; if you tell it your first name, it remembers only that and your last topic, in your own browser's storage on this device, never sent anywhere (type “forget me” to erase it).
