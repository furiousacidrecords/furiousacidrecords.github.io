01 · Origin

The architect

It started as a theoretical-physics sketch: a hypothetical superacid meant to protonate any known element. That became the Purple Rabbit on TikTok in 2020.

The project went viral for a “how it’s made” directness and the original channel was banned for being too direct. Growth since then is unpaid.
