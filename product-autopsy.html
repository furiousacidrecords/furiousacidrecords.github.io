Report structure

The finished report is built around the product—not a generic AI summary.

1. Product identity

Exact product, manufacturer, version, stated use, and the source documents analyzed.

2. Ingredient map

Major ingredients grouped by role: solvent, surfactant, acid/base, oxidizer, binder, fragrance, colorant, or other function.

3. What each part does

Plain-language explanation of why each disclosed ingredient is present and what changes when the formulation differs.

4. Comparison

Relevant differences from one or two comparable products, including formulation strengths and tradeoffs.

5. Compatibility

Material, storage, mixing, ventilation, and handling concerns supported by manufacturer or primary technical sources.

6. Bottom line

A direct answer to the customer’s original question, plus a source list for checking the work.
